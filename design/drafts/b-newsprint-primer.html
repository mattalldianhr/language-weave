How the trick works

Three things to know, and un poco about nouns.

Rare, and placed with care.

Swaps are rare. One word in fifty at first, nunca more than one in twenty. Each one lands where the sentence has ya told you what it means. If it hasn't, the word stays English.

Hover, then click.

Hover a word and the English appears above it. Click it and it flips back. Nothing else on la página moves.

It comes back.

Each word returns on later days. Tomorrow, then next week, then next month. About ten meetings, spaced out, until it sticks. Then it stops coming, porque it is yours. A nuevo one takes its place.

Nouns bring a friend.

A noun arrives with its article: la casa, das Haus. Gender comes for free, and there was nada to memorize.

Notes in the margin

For the grown-ups. The numbers are real.

aon rarityThe 100 most common English words cover 46% of web text, pero they are almost all grammar words you can't swap. A useful list starts around 300 words and is mostly nouns and connectors.

bon rarity, againKeep foreign words under 1 in 20 of the text, nearer 1 in 50, so comprehension stays above 95%.

con placementNaive word-for-word swapping is right only 63% of the time. Part-of-speech checks and a sense whitelist lift that to 82%. The remaining errors are idioms and senses, which a verifier catches.

don coming backA word needs about 8–12 meetings before it stays. Fewer than 8, and nothing was recalled three months later.

eon articlesLearning a noun sin its article makes learning its gender harder later.

What leaves your machine

Page text stays on your machine, siempre.

When your browser has on-device models, those do the work.

A cloud key is used only if you add one, and then only sentence fragments are sent.
