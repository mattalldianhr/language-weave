Language Weave settings

Everything here lives in your browser's extension storage. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you add a cloud key, and then only the sentences being checked.

Reading

Research puts comfortable reading between 1 in 50 and 1 in 20. The default is 1 in 33. Any value is honoured exactly; 1 means every eligible word.

How much do you already know?

Marks the most common words as known so they stop being introduced. Never lowers progress you already have.

Sites

Sign-in, checkout, billing, and account pages are always skipped.

Checking swaps

Without anything set up, swaps come from word rules that were right about nine times in ten in testing. Chrome's on-device model or translator, when present, check sense and form. A cloud key does the same on any machine.

Your progress

Privacy

Pages are read and rewritten inside your browser. Progress, settings, and any key stay in extension storage on this device. There is no account, no analytics, and no server of ours. With a cloud key, the sentences that contain a candidate word are sent to that provider and nothing else; the daily limit above caps those calls.
